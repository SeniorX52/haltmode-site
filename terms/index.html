Terms and refunds

These terms cover the Halt app for Windows, its browser extension and Halt Pro. They are written to be read. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

The short version

  • Halt Basic is free. Halt Pro is one payment, with every update to Halt for Windows included. There is no subscription.
  • One license is for you, on up to 3 of your PCs.
  • Not happy? Ask within 30 days of buying and you get all your money back.
  • Only install Halt on a computer you own or have the right to manage.

Halt Basic and the free trial

Halt Basic is free to download and use, for as long as you like. The first time Halt is installed on a PC, Halt Pro is unlocked for 7 days, with no card and no account. The trial runs once per PC. When it ends, Halt keeps working as Halt Basic, and the Pro settings you made are kept for when you add a license.

Halt Pro

Halt Pro is a one-time purchase. It gives you a license key for Halt for Windows that keeps working for good, with every future update of Halt for Windows included at no extra cost.

  • The license is for you, the person who bought it. You can use it on up to 3 PCs you own or use, and move it between PCs from Halt's settings.
  • Please don't share, publish or resell your key. A key that turns up on many different PCs is paused until we check it, and the PCs already using it keep working.
  • Halt for Mac and the apps for Android and iPhone are not out yet. When they are, we will say clearly how they are offered.

Paying

Halt Pro is sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record: it takes the payment, adds sales tax or VAT where your country requires it, and handles refunds. Your payment is also covered by Polar's terms. Prices are in US dollars.

Refunds: 30 days, no questions

If Halt Pro isn't right for you, write to support@haltmode.com within 30 days of buying, from the email you paid with or with your order number, and we refund the full price. A refunded license stops working on all your PCs, and Halt goes back to Halt Basic. A payment that is charged back through your bank is treated the same way.

Using Halt

  • Halt changes settings on your PC to do its job: browser policies, your network's DNS while the adult filter is on, a small Windows service, and, if you turn it on, access to Task Manager and the Command Prompt during a session. Uninstalling Halt puts them back. Our FAQ explains each one.
  • Only install Halt on a computer you own or have the right to manage, and don't use it to watch or control someone without their knowledge where the law doesn't allow it. Halt doesn't record what anyone browses.
  • Locks are designed to be hard to undo on purpose. An administrator can always reverse Halt's changes, and Windows Safe Mode always gives you a way back into your PC.
