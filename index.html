Features

What Halt does

Most blockers are easy to turn off the moment you're tempted. With Halt, you decide in advance how hard that should be.

Works in every browser

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, including private windows. Halt uses the same browser settings that schools and companies use, so there is no extension to remove.

Blocks apps and games

Choose from the apps on your PC, like Steam games, Valorant, Discord or WhatsApp. If you open one during a session, Halt closes it within a minute.

You choose how strict

With the timer lock, you can't stop until the time is up. The other locks let you stop early after a wait, a long random text, a restart, or a password that a friend keeps.

Hard to get around

A small Windows service puts the block back if something removes it. It keeps working after a restart, changing the clock doesn't end it, and you can't uninstall Halt during a session.

Adult content filter

Blocks adult sites on the whole PC and locks SafeSearch, using Cloudflare's family filter. To turn it off, you wait out a cool-down that you set in advance.

Schedules

Set blocks to start on their own, for example every weekday from 9 to 1. They run even when Halt's window is closed.

Why it's called Halt

Named after a check from addiction recovery

Before acting on an urge, ask yourself if you're hungry, angry, lonely or tired. Often the urge comes from one of those, and it fades if you wait. Halt makes you wait.

Questions

Which computers does Halt work on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. There's no Mac or phone version yet.

Can I stop a session early?

Only in the way your lock allows. With the timer lock you can't. With the other locks you can, after a wait, a long random text, a restart or a password.

Does Halt see what I browse?

No. Halt has no account and no servers, and your lists, stats and settings stay on your PC. Two outside services are used: site logos come from DuckDuckGo's icon service, and the adult filter uses Cloudflare's family DNS, which sees the website names your PC looks up, like any DNS service.

What does Halt change on my PC, and what happens when I uninstall?

Browser policies for the sites you block, your network's DNS while the adult filter is on, and one small Windows service. Uninstalling puts all of it back. While a session is running or the adult filter is on, Halt can't be uninstalled, so you can't remove it on impulse.

Will it slow down my PC?

No. The Halt service runs a quick check once a minute and does nothing the rest of the time.

Could someone determined get around it?

Yes. An administrator can always undo changes on their own PC. We keep it that way so a bug can never lock you out of your computer. Halt makes giving in slow and annoying, and for most people that is enough.
