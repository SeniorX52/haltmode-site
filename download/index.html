Installing it

  1. If Edge says "Halt-Setup.exe isn't commonly downloaded", keep the file: see the pictures below. Browsers say this about new apps that few people have downloaded yet.
  2. Open Halt-Setup.exe from your downloads and follow the steps. Halt installs a small Windows service, which is what keeps your blocks in place.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway (pictures below). Halt is code-signed, so you can check who published it.
  4. Halt opens by itself. Add the sites and apps you want to block, pick a lock, and press Start. Your browsers pick up the block within seconds.
