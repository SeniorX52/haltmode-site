Halt vs BlockerX

BlockerX is a popular adult content blocker. Its main apps are for Android and iPhone, and it has an extension for Chrome. Halt is a Windows app that blocks adult sites in every browser and app on the PC. This page sets out where they differ, including where BlockerX is the better choice.

Pick BlockerX if

  • most of your trouble is on your phone
  • you want an accountability partner who gets reports
  • you want a partner to be told if the app is removed

Pick Halt if

  • you need your Windows PC covered in every browser and app, not only Chrome
  • you'd rather pay once than every year
  • you want turning the filter off to wait for a cool-down you set in advance

Where BlockerX is ahead

Phones are where many people struggle most, and BlockerX is built for them. Halt doesn't run on phones at all. BlockerX also offers an accountability partner, which helps people who want someone else involved. If your phone is the problem, BlockerX is the better choice.

Where Halt is different

The whole PC, not one browser

Halt filters at the network level, so Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox and every other app on the PC are covered, including private windows. Changing a browser's secure DNS setting doesn't get around it.

One payment

Halt Pro costs $29 once, with no subscription.

A cool-down you set in advance

Instead of telling someone, Halt makes you wait. Turning the filter off takes the cool-down you picked when you were thinking clearly, and with Permanent, it takes 30 days or an unlock code from support.

Questions

Is Halt a good BlockerX alternative?

On a Windows PC, yes. Halt blocks adult sites in every browser and app on the PC, not only in Chrome, and you pay once. On a phone, BlockerX is the better choice, because Halt works only on Windows.

Does Halt have an accountability partner?

No. Halt doesn't send reports to anyone. It makes turning the filter off wait for a cool-down you chose in advance instead.

Can I use Halt on my PC and BlockerX on my phone?

Yes. They run on different devices, so they don't affect each other.
