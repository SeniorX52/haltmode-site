Halt vs Covenant Eyes

Covenant Eyes and Halt both keep adult content off your computer, but they work in different ways. Covenant Eyes, through its Victory app, is built around accountability: it takes screenshots and shares them with a person you choose. Halt doesn't watch or report anything. It blocks adult sites on your PC and makes turning the filter off slow. This page sets out where they differ, including where Covenant Eyes is the better choice.

Pick Covenant Eyes if

  • you want another person to see your activity and talk with you about it
  • you need your phone, tablet or Mac covered too
  • you want a faith-based program, or one plan for a family

Pick Halt if

  • you want the block itself to hold, without anyone seeing your screen
  • you'd rather pay once than every month
  • you also want distracting sites, apps and games blocked while you work or study

Where Covenant Eyes is ahead

For many people, talking honestly with another person is what makes change last, and Covenant Eyes is built for exactly that. It also covers phones, tablets and Macs, which Halt doesn't, and one plan can cover a whole family. If you want someone else involved, or most of your trouble is on your phone, Covenant Eyes is the better choice.

Where Halt is different

Nobody watches

Halt takes no screenshots and sends no reports. Some people don't want anyone, even a trusted friend, to see their screen. For them, a block that holds on its own is the better fit.

One payment

Halt Pro costs $29 once. At $18 a month, Covenant Eyes costs more than that within two months.

A cool-down you set in advance

Turning Halt's filter off waits out a cool-down you picked when you were thinking clearly. With Permanent, it takes 30 days or an unlock code from support. While the filter is on, Halt can't be uninstalled.

Also a focus blocker

The same app blocks the sites, apps and games you waste time on, for set focus sessions or on a schedule.

Questions

Is Halt a good Covenant Eyes alternative?

If you want adult sites blocked on a Windows PC without anyone seeing your screen, and you'd rather pay once, yes. If you want a partner to see your activity, or you need your phone covered, Covenant Eyes is the better choice.

Is Halt an accountability app?

No. Halt doesn't take screenshots or send reports to anyone. It blocks adult sites on your PC and makes turning the filter off wait for a cool-down you chose in advance.

Does Halt work on my phone?

No. Halt works on Windows 10 and 11. See the adult filter page for simple ways to filter a phone.
