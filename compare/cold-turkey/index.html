Halt vs Cold Turkey

Cold Turkey has been blocking distractions since 2010 and is one of the strictest blockers you can buy. Halt is newer and works only on Windows. Both block websites and apps and make it hard to give up early. This page sets out where they differ, including where Cold Turkey is the better choice.

Pick Cold Turkey if

  • you also use a Mac
  • you want to lock the whole computer, not only sites and apps
  • you want an app with many years of reviews behind it

Pick Halt if

  • you want adult sites blocked on the whole PC, with safe search locked and a cool-down you set in advance
  • you want blocking that doesn't depend on browser extensions
  • you use Windows only and want the free version to include a lock

Where Cold Turkey is ahead

If you switch between Windows and a Mac, Cold Turkey covers both with one purchase, and Halt can't. Cold Turkey can also block the whole computer, which Halt doesn't do. And it has had sixteen years of fixes and reviews. If any of these matter most to you, Cold Turkey is the better buy, and we would rather say so here than have you find out later.

Where Halt is different

Adult content is blocked on the whole PC

Halt's adult filter points the whole PC at Cloudflare's family filter, which Cloudflare keeps up to date. It works in every browser and every app, not only in the browsers that have an extension. Safe search stays locked on in Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo. Turning the filter off takes a cool-down you choose in advance, from 30 minutes to 7 days, so a late-night urge passes before anything changes. With Permanent, only Halt support can turn it off early.

No extensions to manage

Halt sets the block through the policy settings that Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox already have for schools and companies. There is nothing to install in each browser, and nothing to switch off in a weak moment.

A lock in the free version

Halt Basic includes the Timer lock: once a session starts, it runs to the end. It keeps working after a restart, and changing the clock doesn't end it.

Questions

Is Halt a good Cold Turkey alternative?

If you only use Windows, yes. Both block websites and apps and make it hard to stop early. Halt adds a filter that blocks adult sites on the whole PC with safe search locked, and it blocks sites through the browsers' own settings instead of extensions. Cold Turkey is the better choice if you also use a Mac or want to lock the whole computer.

Does Halt work on a Mac?

No. Halt works on Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.

Does Halt need a browser extension?

No. Halt uses the policy settings built into Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, the same ones schools and companies use. There is no extension to switch off, and private windows are covered.

How much does Halt cost?

Halt Basic is free for as long as you like. Halt Pro is $29, paid once, from January 2027, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
