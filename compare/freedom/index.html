Halt vs Freedom

Freedom blocks distractions on your computer and your phone and keeps one session running across all of them. Halt does one thing: it blocks on your Windows PC, with no account, for a single payment. Which one fits you depends mostly on whether you need your phone covered.

Pick Freedom if

  • you want your phone or tablet blocked too
  • you want one session to run on all your devices at once
  • you use a Mac or a Chromebook

Pick Halt if

  • your PC is where you lose the most time
  • you want to pay once, $29, with no subscription and no account
  • you want adult sites blocked on the whole PC, with safe search locked and a cool-down you set in advance

Where Freedom is ahead

Freedom covers your phone, tablet, Mac and Chromebook as well as Windows, and one session can run on all of them together. If your phone is where most of your time goes, Halt can't help with that, and Freedom is the better choice. It also has years of use by millions of people behind it.

Where Halt is different

One payment, no account

Halt Pro is $29 once. There is no account to make and nothing is stored on a server: your lists, stats and settings stay on your PC.

Adult content is blocked on the whole PC

Halt's adult filter points the whole PC at Cloudflare's family filter, which Cloudflare keeps up to date, so it works in every browser and every app. Safe search stays locked on. Turning the filter off takes a cool-down you choose in advance, from 30 minutes to 7 days, so a late-night urge passes before anything changes. With Permanent, only Halt support can turn it off early.

A lock in the free version

Halt Basic includes the Timer lock: once a session starts, it runs to the end. It keeps working after a restart, and changing the clock doesn't end it.

Questions

Is Halt a good Freedom alternative?

If you only need your Windows PC blocked, yes. Halt costs $29 once instead of a subscription, needs no account, and has a filter that blocks adult sites on the whole PC. Freedom is the better choice if you also want your phone or tablet blocked, or one session running on all your devices.

Does Halt block my phone?

No. Halt works on Windows 10 and Windows 11 only.

Does Halt need an account?

No. Halt has no account and no servers. Your lists, stats and settings stay on your PC.

Is Halt a subscription?

No. Halt Basic is free for as long as you like. Halt Pro is $29, paid once, from January 2027, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
