Daily limits

Cap how long you spend on a site, an app or a whole bundle each day, for example YouTube for 2 hours or Spotify for 6. When you reach the cap it is blocked for the rest of the day, and only the lock you choose can lift it early. A site counts while it's the tab in front of you. An app counts while it's in front, or while it plays sound, like music. Time away from the PC doesn't count, and everything resets at midnight.
